Open or start a DCAT-US 3.0 catalog

Edit a DCAT-US 3.0 catalog in your browser: browse it, fix it, check it against the schema, and download the result.

Your catalog stays in this browser

Files you open are not uploaded, and nothing you enter is sent anywhere. The editor keeps your working copy in this browser on this device only. It is not backed up, and it is lost if you clear this browser’s site data. To keep your work, export it. More about where your data lives
