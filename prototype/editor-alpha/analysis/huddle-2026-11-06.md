# Team huddle, 2026-11-06: decisions and impact on epic #15

The team reviewed the first round of these mockups in a huddle on November 6, 2026. This note is based on the raw auto-transcript, which has no speaker labels. James (jbrown-xentity) leads product and architecture, so where he made a call, that call is the decision. Other people's ideas are recorded as ideas.

This is a working note for Cody. **No GitHub issue has been edited.** The "Impact on sub-issues" section lists the changes to make by hand.

## Ideas raised but not decided

These are listed as open questions in SPEC.md. None of them are built.

- **Working set:** let the user pick the handful of datasets they are working on and show only those ("at any given moment, someone's probably working on, like, 5 data sets"). Reed and others raised it. James preferred to measure first.
- **Lazy loading:** load only part of a large catalog from IndexedDB, then more as the user searches. James: "there may come a time", but for 2 MB "the browser will probably be able to handle it".
- **Lower limits or splitting:** cap what the public alpha edits, or tell very large agencies to split their catalog.
- **Tabs for several open objects:** asked about, and answered with "one form at a time" plus a strong tree highlight.
- **Simplifying the repeated contact:** James hoped the IndexedDB copy "would simplify" a contact written out 1,800 times. That conflicts with #20, which says to never merge identical objects.

## Out of the alpha's scope (beta and later)

These came up in the discussion but are not alpha work:

- Logged-in users and shared catalogs, along the lines of Touchpoints.
- Sub-catalogs from other teams nested into an agency catalog.
- An editor role.
- Publishing and harvesting directly from inventory.
